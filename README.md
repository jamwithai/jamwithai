### How we teach

Follow a complete project from the first design choices through testing and deployment. Our repositories show the architecture, working code, and trade-offs behind AI systems you can inspect and run.

### Open-source projects

| Project | What you can explore |
|---|---|
| [Production Agentic RAG Course](https://github.com/jamwithai/production-agentic-rag-course) | Build a research assistant with data ingestion, hybrid search, agents, APIs, tracing, and monitoring. |
